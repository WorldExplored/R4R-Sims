## Timing and criterion interpretation

`audit-config.json` holds all numbers. Takeoff audit evidence: fresh armed telemetry and ≥1 m above initial ground for ≥1 s. Touchdown: fresh `MAV_LANDED_STATE_ON_GROUND`, speed norm ≤0.2 m/s for 2 s, followed by disarmed heartbeat; unavailable landed-state makes confirmation blocked. These are audit settings pending installed telemetry availability, not lab requirements. Keep minimum B integration (confirmed takeoff, two waypoint transitions, landing/disarming) distinct from full-route completion (every leg meets tolerance/dwell + landing/disarming). Report tracking error from estimates separately from estimate–truth disagreement. Do not call command delivery or elapsed sleeps a pass.

Log raw source/image simulation time, PX4 boot time, receiver monotonic and UTC separately. Source meaning and epoch/unit conversion need measurement. Equal pixel hashes at a stationary pose do not alone establish stale frames; use raw sequence/acquisition timestamps. Use bracketing simulator truth at acquisition time, no extrapolation; record interpolation, offsets/residuals, transport delay and association uncertainty. Planned bracket-gap ≤0.1 s and clock residual ≤0.02 s are audit quality gates. Shared-memory SDK fallback computes a start offset using receiver time and a fixed 0.01 s heuristic; it does not measure latency. If acquisition clocks cannot be associated, keep frames but geometric A/coupling remain blocked. Capture throughput, playback FPS, image-call latency, control rate and policy latency are separate. GPU utilization/VRAM polls are not renderer throughput. GPU timing requires verified warmup/synchronization; otherwise report not measured.
