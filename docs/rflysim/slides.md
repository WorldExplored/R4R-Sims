6. **Frozen B route and criteria** — NED metres: origin → (0,0,−2) → (2,0,−2) → (2,2,−2) → (0,0,−2) → land Position error ≤0.25 m for a continuous 2 s dwell at every leg. Target setpoint rate 10 Hz; 75 s flight and separate 15 s cleanup watchdog Minimum integration: takeoff, two waypoint transitions, landing/disarming. Full completion: every prescribed leg meets tolerance/dwell and confirms landing/disarming
7. **A camera calibration and replay** — Seven desired camera poses: baseline at (0,0,−2); +1 m north; +1 m east; 1 m higher; yaw +15°; pitch +10°; baseline again 640×480 RGB, requested 10 Hz, 21 s sequence replayed twice; 5 s warmup. Short rate test: 30 s after 5 s warmup Repeatability: 0.02 m and 1°. Known-geometry reprojection: 2% of image dimension. Clearance, geometry, actual pose and clock mapping still block execution freeze
8. **Flight-to-observation evidence** — Commands → PX4 → dynamics → achieved simulator state → actual camera pose → timestamped RGB B initial run without capture; reset and repeat the same route once with mounted capture if supported. Keep image/GPU/file workers separate from control Record source clocks and receiver clocks, pose brackets, offsets, uncertainty, stale frames, gaps and missed deadlines. Reference poses and PX4 estimates cannot become camera truth
9. **Runner findings** — Recovered runner preserves evidence but cannot enforce deadlines independently of blocking SDK calls PNG saving and GPU queries share the control loop; cached frames may be stale. No waypoint dwell or confirmed touchdown/disarm pass is implemented Current entry point validates preparation and verifies hashes; simulator execution is disabled. Installed adapter, watchdog and capture isolation remain pending
10. **Capture import and fidelity** — Direct real images + poses: no inspected reconstruction loader established Mesh route is documented through UE import, baking/cooking and XML MeshPath indexing. It is not a generic runtime OBJ/PLY loader; Free eligibility remains unresolved Gaussian loader: no supported path established. Sparse capture coverage, altitude extrapolation and held-out fidelity remain blocked by missing calibrated assets
11. **Gaussian renderer boundary** — Pose replay exports a route. Native visual replacement changes the renderer. Closed-loop visual control also requires a compatible observation/action consumer A separate renderer using CopterSim truth and calibrated extrinsics is inferred feasible; native image injection, reset/step and clock ownership are unresolved UE collision and depth services must be retained or replaced with independently validated geometry. Implementation is outside preparation
12. **Policy and task status** — PX4 plus scripted waypoints is a controller, not a released autonomy policy RflyArena publishes state-feedback RL weights: 25-dimensional state/error/reference input and throttle/body-rate output. Default-model and task compatibility are not established No compatible R4R visual policy, scene/task or adapter supplied. C remains blocked; no unrelated weights or training
